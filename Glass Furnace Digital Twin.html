Glass Furnace Digital Twin

An interactive simulator of an industrial soda-lime glass melting furnace. Move any slider and the energy balance, exergy, CO2, melt viscosity and glass properties recalculate instantly. A machine-learning surrogate then searches the operating window for better settings.

  • First law energy balance
  • Second law exergy analysis
  • VFT melt viscosity
  • Gradient boosting surrogate
  • Illustrative parameters, no plant data

How the model works
  • Melt viscosity. Vogel-Fulcher-Tammann law, log₁₀ η = A + B / (T − T₀), with A, B and T₀ shifted by the weight percent of each oxide.
  • Glass transition and liquidus. Tg is a 510 °C baseline plus additive oxide contributions. Liquidus is an empirical soda-lime-silica fit.
  • Energy balance. Sensible heat, latent heat, endothermic batch reactions, wall radiation and flue gas loss, minus the share supplied by electric boosting.
  • Exergy. Chemical exergy of the fuel, physical exergy of the hot glass, and Carnot-weighted radiation and flue losses.
  • CO2. Fuel combustion, carbonate decomposition in the batch, grid electricity at 0.25 kg/kWh, less the saving from recycled cullet.
  • ML surrogate. Three gradient boosting regressors (150 trees, depth 4) learn CO2, energy demand and durability from 400 random simulations, then a bounded search runs on the surrogate instead of the slower physics model.
